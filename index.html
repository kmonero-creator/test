A playful 2-player Nepal Valentine showdown

Player 1: Himalayan Heart

Health: 100

🇳🇵 💌 🇳🇵
🛏️ Valentine Bed

Turn: Himalayan Heart

Tap Shoot or Reload to begin!

How to play

  • Players take turns.
  • Each shot uses 1 ammo and deals random damage (10-25).
  • Reload refills ammo to 6 but ends your turn.
  • First player to reduce the rival to 0 health wins the bed.
